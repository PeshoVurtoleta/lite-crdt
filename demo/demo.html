lite-crdt

Two replicas of one document, each backed by its own CRDT engine. Take a side offline, edit freely, then reorder, duplicate, or replay the operation stream between them. The two states always reconcile to exactly the same result — no central server, no merge conflicts, no lost edits. Every panel re-renders from a lite-signal effect bound to the projection; nothing here calls a render function by hand.
